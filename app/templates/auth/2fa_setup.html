What is Two-Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your account. After entering your password, you'll need to enter a code from your authenticator app.

Important

You'll need an authenticator app like Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator installed on your phone.
